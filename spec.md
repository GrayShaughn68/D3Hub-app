# D3Hub Product Specification

## Product overview

**D3Hub** is a one-stop destination for Division III (D3) sports scores and team information. It helps players, their families, and fans follow D3 teams, rankings, playoff chances, and schedules in one place.

## Problem

Sports score apps tend to focus on professional and Division I teams. D3 players and their families need an accessible way to keep up with their own teams and the wider D3 sports landscape.

## Target users

- Division III student-athletes
- Families of Division III athletes
- Fans interested in Division III teams and sports
- Players who want to follow standings, rankings, playoff chances, or schedules

## Core user need

When a user wants to follow Division III sports, they can find scores, rankings, playoff chances, schedules, and team information in one app and stay current on the teams they care about.

## Core experience

1. The user opens a homepage with Division III sports to choose from.
2. The user selects a sport.
3. The user can view the sport's top 25 teams and navigate to Division III college team pages.
4. The user follows team information, including scores, rankings, playoff chances, and schedules.

## Functional requirements

### Must have

- A homepage that presents the available sports.
- A top 25 team ranking for each supported sport.
- A homepage or profile page for every Division III college sports team.
- Team and sport information focused exclusively on the Division III level.

### Nice to have

- Player statistics.
- Team statistics.

## Out of scope

- Rankings and scores for schools outside Division III, including Division I, Division II, FBS, and FCS.

## Look and feel

- Bold, intense colors.
- Familiar sports-score-app presentation, using ESPN as a style reference.

## Acceptance criteria

- Users can start from a homepage and choose a sport.
- Each supported sport has a top 25 ranking view.
- Each Division III college team has a team page.
- Product content is limited to Division III; non-D3 rankings and scores are not included.
- The interface uses a bold, sports-oriented visual style.

## Open questions

- Which sports are included at launch?
- What data sources will provide scores, schedules, rankings, and team pages?
- How are rankings and playoff chances defined or calculated for each sport?
- How frequently should scores, schedules, and standings be refreshed?
- Should users be able to save or follow teams?
- Which team and player statistics should be shown if statistics are added?
- What platform(s) will the first release support?
